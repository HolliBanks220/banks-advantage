Log the outcome honestly

No answer for a ring-out. Callback when they name a time. Conversation when you spoke but didn't book. Not interested when they're done.

Not interested is final. It closes their case, clears any appointment and takes them out of your queue. It's the right button when they mean it — and the wrong one when they're just busy. That's what Callback is for.

The appointment, start to finish

Everything from sitting down to the application being in.

Log what happened

Open the lead → Log outcome. Five choices, and they mean different things:

Sale · No sale — you sat and showed numbers.
No Show — alive — didn't turn up, still worth working.
No Show — dead — didn't turn up, and you're done.
Cancelled — still live — they called ahead. Stays open, back in your queue.

Cancelled is not a no sale. If they never saw numbers, it was never really an appointment. Logging it as a no sale makes your close ratio a lie.

Know which month you're looking at

The period arrows move you back and forth. But a "month" here is the production month — Saturday after one month's last Friday, through the next month's last Friday. It's the month carriers pay on, not the 1st to the 31st.

So June might start on May 30. A charge you dated June 30 counts in July. Not a bug — it's the calendar your commission runs on.

06

Stuck?

The things that catch everyone. Search at the top of the page finds these too.

A lead disappeared and I can't find them

Search finds leads in any status, including dead ones — type any part of the name or number in the Leads search box. It also matches the name on an application, so a spouse is findable too.

If they're gone because you logged something by mistake, use Settings → History. Pick today and the hour, find the action, tap Undo this. The lead comes back exactly as it was, appointment and all.

The ↺ icon next to the gear undoes the very last thing you did, if it was just now.

I logged Not interested by mistake

Open the lead, find that line in the call log, and remove it. Taking the entry away puts the lead back the way it was — case reopened, appointment restored, status back to what it was. See logging outcomes.

I sold a policy but I can't enter the application

The + Log outcome button only appears on an open case. If every case on that lead is closed, there's nothing for the application to attach to.

Book the appointment again and you'll be offered Reopen a case — it lists every closed case so you can pick the right one. It goes back to open and you can log the sit and the application. See the appointment section.

What's the difference between a reset and a continuation?

A continuation is the same case, next meeting — you sat, and you're going back. Policy pickup, a rewrite, a think-about-it.

A reset is a different product. They said no to mortgage protection but they'll talk about retirement. That opens a new case, and it doesn't undo the first verdict.

They cancelled but want to reschedule — what do I log?

Cancelled — still live. The case stays open, the appointment clears, and they go straight back into your call queue. You'll be offered a callback or a rebook right after.

Don't use no sale — that means they saw your numbers and passed.

An old client is showing an appointment that shouldn't be there

Settings → Repair. It lists anyone in a contradictory state — an appointment on a lead who said no, a live step on a closed case, a callback on someone with nothing open.

It shows what's wrong before it changes anything, and it never touches sales, sits or logged outcomes.

Why did my client disappear from the call list this evening?

Calling curfew. It's by their local time, and by age: 70 and over stops at 7pm, 65 to 69 at 8pm, everyone else at 9:30. Their card reads Too late to call.

An appointment you already booked still shows. It's cold calling that stops.

My commission per lead looks far too high or too low

Check your contract level first — Settings → Profile. Every figure is premium × that number.

Then tap the commission figure in Reports. It lists every application behind it, biggest first, with what each one contributed. One wrong application is usually the whole gap.

Why does a month show numbers from the month before?

It doesn't — you're looking at a production month. July runs from the Saturday after June's last Friday through July's last Friday. A charge dated June 30 lands in July because that's the carrier's July.

What does N/A mean in a report?

No data for that period — usually a period before you started using CWC. It's deliberately not a zero, because zero would read as "you did nothing" rather than "nothing was recorded".

Will I lose everything if I clear my browser?

No. Leads, calls, appointments, applications and settings all live in the database. Log in from another machine and it's all there.

What counts as a sit?

You showed them numbers. That's it. One per case, whatever it took to get there — so a lead you met three times before they decided is still one sit.

A cancellation isn't a sit. A no-show isn't a sit.

Should I be looking at which lead types perform best?

Not yet, and it isn't turned on for you. New agents who study lead quality learn to blame the leads. Learn to work them first — the numbers will still be there in three months, and they'll mean more.
